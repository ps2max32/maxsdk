# IPU

The EE's decoder of MPEG macroblocks (EE User's Manual, ch. 8; `<mx/ipu.h>`, `<mx/mpeg.h>`). What it
is used for and what is to come: [video-plan.md](video-plan.md). PS2: a SCPH-70004.
